  • Step away for 5 minutes and it counts as a break
  • Pause Ebb for 30 min, 1 hour or until you resume

04Exceptions

Doing VIP work? Ask for more time.

Deep in a deadline or about to join a call? Push the break back. A small daily budget stops it from becoming a habit.

  • Choose 5, 10, 15 or 25 more minutes
  • Say why: one quick line is enough
  • 3 per day, and never more than 2 hours straight

05Meeting-aware

Never blocks you in a meeting.

If a break comes due during a call, Ebb waits. It starts the break 90 seconds after you hang up.

  • Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack, FaceTime and more
  • No camera or mic recording — Ebb only checks if they're in use
  • A call coming in pauses a break so you can join

06Strict mode

As gentle or as firm as you need.

Normal mode has an emergency exit. Strict mode removes it, for when you know you'll cheat.

  • Normal: hold for 3 seconds to skip in a real emergency
  • Strict: hides the Dock and menu bar and blocks ⌘‑Tab
  • Exceptions still work in both modes
